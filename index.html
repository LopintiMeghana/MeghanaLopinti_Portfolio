About Me

A fresher with professional mindset, creativity, and ownership.

I’m a Master of Business Analytics graduate with a strong interest in data visualization, dashboarding, and data-driven decision-making. I enjoy taking messy, unstructured information and turning it into clear, visual stories that help people act with confidence.

Even as a fresher, I’ve had exposure to operations, reporting, and product-style work: from supporting documentation and financial reporting in construction and tech environments, to building a beauty services mobile app with a premium, modern interface.
